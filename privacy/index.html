Konten Studio Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Overview

Konten Studio Pro is a local creator tool that helps generate short-form video clips and upload drafts to TikTok after the creator authorizes the app.

Information We Process

The app may process source video metadata, generated clip files, captions, TikTok authorization tokens, and basic TikTok account information required to upload drafts.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to generate video clips, authenticate with TikTok, upload drafts selected by the creator, and show upload status. The app does not sell personal data.

Local Storage

TikTok tokens and generated files are stored locally on the creator's machine unless the creator chooses to move or upload them elsewhere.

Third-Party Services

The app uses TikTok APIs for authorization and draft upload. TikTok's handling of data is governed by TikTok's own terms and privacy policy.

Data Retention

Generated files and local tokens remain on the creator's machine until the creator deletes them or revokes TikTok access.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app owner through the GitHub repository associated with this site.
