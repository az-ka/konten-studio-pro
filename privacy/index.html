Konten Studio Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

Overview

Konten Studio Pro is a creator tool that runs locally on your machine. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it to create and upload video content to TikTok.

Information We Collect

The app may collect and process the following information:

  • Source video URLs: You provide a URL to a video you wish to process.
  • Generated clips and captions: Short-form clips and caption files created by the app.
  • TikTok authorization tokens: Temporary tokens received through TikTok OAuth to upload drafts on your behalf.
  • Basic TikTok account info: Limited account information required to verify authorization and upload drafts.

How We Use Information

Information is used exclusively to operate the app's core functions: generate video clips, authenticate with TikTok, upload drafts you select, and display upload status. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Local Storage

TikTok authorization tokens and generated files are stored locally on your machine. You may delete them at any time by revoking TikTok access or removing the files manually.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties except as required to provide the service. Generated clips are uploaded to TikTok only when you explicitly choose to upload a draft. TikTok's handling of your data is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The app integrates with TikTok APIs for authorization and draft upload. We are not responsible for TikTok's data practices. Please review TikTok's Privacy Policy for more information.

Data Retention

Generated files and local tokens remain on your machine until you delete them or revoke TikTok access. TikTok authorization tokens are stored temporarily and may be revoked at any time through your TikTok account settings.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or delete any data processed by the app. Since all data is stored locally on your machine, you can exercise these rights at any time by deleting the files or revoking TikTok authorization.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can investigate.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, please contact the app owner through the GitHub repository associated with this site or reach out to the repository maintainer directly on GitHub.
